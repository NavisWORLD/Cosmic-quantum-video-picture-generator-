# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in COSMOS Quantum Media.

## Contribution rights policy

This repository contains Cory-owned material being developed under a restricted commercial rights strategy. External contributions are **not accepted for incorporation into proprietary Cory-owned material unless a written contribution, assignment, or other rights agreement is completed first**.

Opening an issue, discussion, or pull request does not transfer copyright ownership and does not by itself grant Cory Shane Davis / NavisWORLD a right to relicense your contribution.

If you want to contribute code, artwork, datasets, prompts, media, documentation, or other copyrightable material, contact Cory Shane Davis / @NavisWORLD before submitting it so the applicable contribution terms can be agreed in writing.

## Research reports and factual feedback

Bug reports, reproducibility reports, benchmark results, security reports, documentation corrections, and citations are welcome. Do not include code, datasets, model weights, confidential information, private media, or other material you do not have the right to submit.

Quantum-performance claims should include matched classical controls and enough technical detail to reproduce the comparison where practical.

See `LICENSE`, `LICENSE_HISTORY.md`, and `COMMERCIAL_RIGHTS.md` before using or contributing material.
