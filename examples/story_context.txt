Mira is a quiet student who notices patterns other people miss. Behind the school greenhouse she finds a seed that gives off a faint blue light whenever someone nearby is kind. Her class plants it in a cracked clay pot. Over the semester the plant grows through the greenhouse roof, and every leaf glows with a different memory the students chose to share. On the last day of school, the class carries one fallen glowing leaf home each, while the tree remains behind for the next group of students to discover.
